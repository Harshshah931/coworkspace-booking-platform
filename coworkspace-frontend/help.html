How can we help?

Find quick answers about booking workspaces, managing reservations, your account and payments. This build records reservations, but it does not process online payments.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a reservation?

Sign in or create an account, browse available workspaces, open a workspace, choose your dates and an available desk or cabin, review the estimated price, then confirm. The server checks availability before saving the reservation.

Does confirming a reservation charge my card?

No. This version does not have a connected payment gateway. Reservations are saved in the application, but no money is collected online. The Payment Preferences page stores only a non-sensitive label in your current browser.

How do I check or cancel a reservation?

Open My Bookings to see your reservations and statuses. For an active reservation, select Cancel and confirm the action. Once cancellation succeeds, the unit can become available for overlapping dates again.

Why can't I reserve a unit I just saw as available?

Another customer may have reserved it since you loaded the availability map. Availability is checked again during confirmation. Choose another unit or refresh availability and retry.

How do I update my profile or password?

Open Profile from the customer navigation. You can update your name and optional contact phone number, and change your password by providing your current password.

Can I manage workspaces as a customer?

Workspace creation, editing, deletion and seat-unit management are restricted to an administrator account. Regular customers can browse and book workspaces and manage only their own reservations.
